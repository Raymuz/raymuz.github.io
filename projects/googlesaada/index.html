Product scope

GoogleSaada brings fixture exploration, football research, model-based forecasting, recommendations and match results into one research-oriented workflow. The goal is a traceable prediction process, not unsupported betting certainty.

calendar-search

Fixture Explorer

Browse scheduled matches, filter by date, competition and teams, and add eligible selections to My Bet Basket. Provider fixture coverage is different from model eligibility; browsing alone should not use prediction credits.

brain-circuit

Model architecture

The project maintains a 13-model registry: ten original models and three worldwide models. Ordered feature contracts, artifact fingerprints, native invocation bridges, common-fixture testing and calibration gates are part of the development audit. A registry check is not the same as a successful live forecast.

My Bet Basket

The saved fixture basket is designed to support add, deduplicate and remove actions across the interface. Prediction and credit workflows belong in an explicitly supported prediction flow, not ordinary fixture browsing.

User guide

Discover fixtures, choose eligible matches, add them to the basket, review available model evidence and interpret recommendations carefully. Explanations should distinguish research-only views from verified predictions and describe credit rules and limitations accurately.
